Flying Laser Marking Machine

Overview

Flying Laser Marking Machine

The flying online laser marking machine is specially designed for assembly line marking and used with assembly line workbenches to realize mass production, save labor costs, and improve work efficiency. This machine is especially suitable for marking on product which is not easy to be positioned by hand, such as: electronic components, circuit boards, resistors and metal buttons. It supports functions such as automatic encoding, serial number, batch number, date, barcode, two-dimensional code, automatic number skipping, etc, to meet the needs of customers in different industries. The continuous movement of the conveyor allows for rapid marking of products, making them well-suited for high-throughput production lines. This adaptability makes them suitable for use in different industries including manufacturing, electronics and automotive.

Advantages

Why this machine earns its place

01

Highlight

High-Speed Marking: The continuous movement of the conveyor allows for rapid marking of products, making them well-suited for high-throughput production lines. This results in increased efficiency and faster overall processing times. Consistent Marking Quality: Flying laser marking machines maintain consistent marking quality across a range of products. The synchronized movement of the laser head with the conveyor ensures that markings are applied with precision and uniformity. Versatility in Marking Applications: These machines are versatile and can be used for marking various materials and shapes.. This adaptability makes them suitable for use in different industries, including manufacturing, electronics, and automotive. Automation Integration: Flying laser marking machines seamlessly integrate into automated production lines. They can be synchronized with other machinery and controlled through programmable logic controllers (PLCs). This integration enhances the overall automation process, reduces manual intervention, and ensures a smooth workflow. Reduced Downtime: The continuous, non-stop operation of flying laser marking machines contributes to reduced downtime in production. The ability to mark products on the fly eliminates the need for frequent stops and starts in the production line, optimizing efficiency and minimizing interruptions.

02

Permanent & Wear-Proof

Laser marks never fade, smudge or wear off, meeting ISO traceability requirements without any ink or consumable.

03

High-Speed Throughput

High-speed galvanometer scanning marks at production line pace, improving productivity and reducing labour.

04

Zero Maintenance

Fiber laser sources deliver over 100,000 hours of service life with no routine maintenance or consumables.
