Overview

3In1 Laser Welding Machine

A 3-in-1 laser welding machine is a cutting-edge and multi functional Industrial tool that integrates three essential laser processes into a single, cohesive platform. This versatile machine encompasses laser welding, laser cleaning, and laser cutting functionalities, providing manufacturers with a comprehensive solution for various production needs. In the realm of laser welding, the machine employs a focused laser beam to precisely melt and fuse materials together, creating strong and intricate welds on metals and alloys. The laser cleaning capability involves non-contact surface cleaning, where the laser beam effectively removes contaminants, rust, paint, and undesired layers without the use of chemicals or abrasive materials. The laser cutting feature utilizes a focused laser beam to make precise cuts on metals offering high-speed and accurate profiling without the need for physical tools. The machine's precision and efficiency make it a valuable asset across diverse industries such as automotive, aerospace, electronics, and more.

Advantages

Why this machine earns its place

01

Highlight

Multi-Functionality and Versatility: A 3-in-1 laser welding machine provides the versatility of three essential functions in a single system—laser welding, laser cutting, and laser cleaning. This multi-functionality allows operators to perform a diverse range of tasks, from precision welding and cutting to surface preparation and marking, all using a single, integrated machine. Space and Cost Savings: Combining multiple functionalities into a single machine results in space and cost savings for users. Instead of investing in separate machines for laser cutting, laser cleaning, and laser marking, a 3-in-1 system optimizes floor space and reduces the overall equipment costs, making it a more economical and space-efficient solution. Precision and Consistency: The precision inherent in laser technology is maintained across all three functions—welding, cutting, and cleaning. This ensures consistent and high-quality results, whether it's creating fine welds, achieving precise cuts, or cleaning surfaces with accuracy. The ability to maintain precision across diverse applications is a significant advantage for users in various industries.

02

Streamlined Workflow and Efficiency

The ability to switch between welding, cutting, and cleaning functions within a single machine streamlines the workflow, eliminating the logistical challenges associated with moving workpieces between different equipment. This integration results in time savings, increased production throughput, and a more efficient utilization of resources. Wide Range of Applications: The 3-in-1 laser welding machine is suitable for a wide range of applications across different industries. From manufacturing and fabrication to maintenance and repair tasks, this versatile machine can handle various materials and meet the requirements of tasks such as welding complex structures, cutting intricate patterns, and cleaning surfaces for optimal adhesion or coating.

03

Wobble Welding Head

Self-developed wobble head widens the weld seam and improves fault tolerance, giving beautiful welds even for new operators.

04

Low Heat Distortion

Concentrated energy means a small heat-affected zone, so thin sheet stays flat with no burn-through.
